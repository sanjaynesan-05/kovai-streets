Add Tamil-first voice system with AI4Bharat TTS generator

- js/lines.js: all 83 story lines (Act 1, Act 2, intro and after-story talk)
  with speaker, Tamil and English text and mood, used by the game and the generator
- js/voice.js: plays recordings listed in assets/voice/manifest.json; a line
  without a recording shows as subtitles only
- Tamil is the default voice and subtitle language; English is optional
- New settings in the main menu and pause menu: voice language (Tamil/English),
  subtitles (Tamil + small English / Tamil only / English only) and dialogue volume
- Subtitles and phone talks show Tamil with smaller English underneath
- Radio volume drops while a character is speaking; voices pause with the game
- Act 1 missions now use the shared dialogue lines (stTalk) instead of inline text
- tools/voice: generate.py (Indic Parler-TTS on a local GPU, ffmpeg to MP3),
  voices.json (one voice per character, with effects for the Architect and the
  police radio) and a setup README
- assets/voice: manifest and generated Tamil/English MP3s
- .gitignore: exclude the .venv-voice environment
